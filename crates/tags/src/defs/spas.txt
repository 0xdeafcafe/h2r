# spas: shader pass. The engine reads only the compiled postprocess (+0x1c):
# implementations with the embedded D3DPIXELSHADERDEF and vrtx reference,
# texture bindings, render and sampler states, variant combiner pieces and
# engine-driven bindings (docs/formats/shader-instances.md "spas"; crates/tags/src/shader.rs).
# Ranges are packed u16: first element in the low 9 bits, count in the high 7.
group spas 0x24 align 4
